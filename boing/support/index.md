## Boing Support

Boing is made by Kraig Wastlund. Questions, bugs and ideas are welcome.

### Contact
Email [v2kapps@gmail.com](mailto:v2kapps@gmail.com?subject=Boing%20support)  

Quickest way to report a bug: in Boing open **Settings > Send Feedback**. It includes your device model, iOS and app
version and a few game details (like your best round) so I can reproduce the problem. Please don't put personal information in the message.

### FAQ

**How do I play?**  
Pull the ball back to aim, then let go. The balls bounce through the bricks and the bricks drop a row each round, so
clear what you can before they reach the bottom. Pink bricks give you bomb balls. On a long turn, tap the board to speed it up,
or tap Recall to bring every ball home.

**Is anything locked behind a purchase?**  
No. Boing is free with no ads. Ball skins are cosmetic only (look and sound; every skin plays exactly the same) and tips unlock nothing.

**I bought a skin on another device, or reinstalled. How do I get it back?**  
Settings > Ball Skins > **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I get a refund?**  
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**What are Crash Reports?**  
Settings > Crash Reports sends crash and diagnostic data to help fix bugs. It is on by default and you can switch it off any time.
See the [privacy policy](/boing/privacy).

**Which languages does Boing support?**  
English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.

**Which devices?**  
iPhone and iPad (portrait), iOS 17 or later.
